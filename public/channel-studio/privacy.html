Privacy policy

Channel Studio · last updated 9 November 2026

Channel Studio is a private program that its author, Debakar Roy, runs on the author’s own computer to publish videos to the author’s own YouTube channel and Facebook Pages. This policy explains what the program accesses and stores.

Information the program accesses

  • Google / YouTube: when the account owner signs in, the program receives a token that lets it upload videos, thumbnails and caption files to that account’s YouTube channel, and read the channel name and the status of videos it uploaded. It requests only the YouTube upload and YouTube management permissions needed for this.
  • Facebook: when the Page admin signs in, the program receives access tokens for the Pages that admin chooses. It uses them only to upload and schedule videos and captions on those Pages and to read the status of those uploads.

Where information is stored

Everything is stored on the computer where the program runs. Sign-in tokens and app credentials are held in the operating system’s secure credential store (the macOS Keychain). Video ideas, schedules and logs are held in a database file on the same computer. No copy is kept on any server operated by the author.

Sharing and use

Information obtained from Google or Facebook is used only to provide the publishing features described above. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train machine-learning models. No analytics or tracking is used. The only data that leaves the computer is the content the author schedules for upload to YouTube and Facebook, sent directly to those services.

YouTube API Services

Channel Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Channel Studio with a YouTube account you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Channel Studio’s access to your Google data at any time in the Google security settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Channel Studio’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Google: remove Channel Studio at myaccount.google.com/permissions. In the program, choose Disconnect on the channel to delete the stored token.
  • Facebook: remove the app under Settings & privacy → Business integrations on Facebook, and choose Disconnect in the program.
  • Deleting the program’s data folder and its Keychain entries removes everything it stored.

Children

The program is not directed to children and has no users other than its author.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.

Terms

Use of Channel Studio is also covered by its terms of service.
