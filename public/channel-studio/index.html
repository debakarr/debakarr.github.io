Channel Studio

A private tool for scheduling videos to my own YouTube channel and Facebook Pages.

What it is

Channel Studio is a small program that runs on one computer (the author’s Mac). It keeps a list of video ideas, holds finished videos with their captions and thumbnails, and uploads them to the author’s own YouTube channel and Facebook Pages at times the author chooses.

What it does with Google and Facebook accounts

  • YouTube: with the account owner’s permission it uploads videos, thumbnails and caption files to the account owner’s own channel, and reads the status of those uploads to confirm they were published.
  • Facebook: with the Page admin’s permission it uploads and schedules videos and captions on Pages that the admin manages.

It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own. Sign-in tokens stay on the computer it runs on. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the author or anyone else, other than the uploads to YouTube and Facebook that the author schedules.
